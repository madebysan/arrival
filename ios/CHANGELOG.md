# Changelog

## 1.1 (2026-10-17)

- Service alerts now expand: tap the banner on a stop to read the full MTA notice and any other alerts for that line, instead of a headline cut off after two lines. The Mac menu bar app does the same on click, scrolling long notices.
- New name on the App Store, Arrival: NYC Subway Times, and a new app icon: subway line-colour dots on black.
- Added privacy manifests to the app and the widget. Both declare UserDefaults use (the app's own settings and the App Group shared with the widget), no tracking and no data collected, which App Store Connect requires for new uploads.

## 1.0 (2026-05-01)

First App Store release: [Arrival on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/arrival-nyc-subway/id6759941751).

- Live arrivals for every NYC subway line from the MTA's realtime feeds, refreshed every 60 seconds
- Up to four saved stops, reordered by drag
- Walking time per stop, highlighting the trains you can still catch
- Home screen widgets that update in the background
- Direction filters and MTA service alerts
- No account and no analytics; saved stops stay on the device

# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026-05-01)

First App Store release: [Arrival on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/arrival-nyc-subway/id6759941751).

- Live arrivals for every NYC subway line from the MTA's realtime feeds, refreshed every 60 seconds
- Up to four saved stops, reordered by drag
- Walking time per stop, highlighting the trains you can still catch
- Home screen widgets that update in the background
- Direction filters and MTA service alerts
- No account and no analytics; saved stops stay on the device
